Master Kaito

“Easy. I will play with my eyes closed.”Kaito, when you pick Easy

Sit at a full-size table in a quiet dojo and play a real match against Kaito, a retired champion who has no patience for bad footwork. Swing the controller like a paddle. The angle and speed of your swing decide where the ball goes.

A real rally

Bounce, spin and the net behave like the real thing. Brush up on the ball for topspin.

He has opinions

Kaito reacts to every point, streak and deuce with his own dry remarks.

Made for sitting

Play from a chair. Move the table up, down, closer or back until it feels right.

Meet your opponent

Moriyama Makoto was born in 1959 in Shinsekai, Osaka, and learned the game against old men in the back room of a shogi parlour, playing for coins.

The table tennis world named him Kaitō, the phantom thief, because his serves stole points. Foreign players heard “Kaito”. He stopped correcting them in the 1990s.

How to play

PaddleYour right controller is the paddle. Hold it like a handle. Left-handed players can switch hands in the menu.
ServeThe ball sits on your free hand. Pull either Trigger to toss it, then hit it.
ReturnSwing as you would with a real paddle. Brushing up gives topspin.
PauseB or Y, or the Quest menu.
MatchTo 11, win by two. Serve changes every two points, then every point from 10 all.

How it was made

Kaito is built from open MakeHuman character assets and brought to life in the browser with Meta's Immersive Web SDK and three.js. His swing, hands and reactions follow the rally as it happens.

The dojo is modelled in code with its lantern light baked into the textures, so it stays smooth on the headset.

Your privacy

No account, no sign-in and no microphone. The game keeps your settings, like table height and hand, on your headset only. This site uses no cookies or analytics.
